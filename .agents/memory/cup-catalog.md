---
name: Cup catalog policy
description: Owner's rule for handling duplicate cup designs in photo uploads.
---
Keep only one cup product per distinct design: «إذا في أكواب مكررات شيلهم بس كوب من كل شكل».

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested no duplicate cup designs.

**How to apply:** Compare new cup photos with existing designs before adding products; update matching products rather than creating duplicates.
