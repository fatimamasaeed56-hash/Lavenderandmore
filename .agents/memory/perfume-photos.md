---
name: Perfume photo policy
description: Owner's instruction for multiple photos of the same perfume.
---
For the same perfume photographed from two different angles, use one photo in the main catalog and switch to the second photo when customers open the perfume details. Do not create a duplicate product for the alternate angle.

**Why:** The owner requested this arrangement explicitly.

**How to apply:** Compare perfume uploads by product identity and angle when assembling the catalog. Identical duplicate photos need only one copy.
