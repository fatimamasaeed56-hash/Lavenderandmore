---
name: Perfume photo policy
description: Owner's instruction for multiple photos of the same perfume.
---
For the same perfume photographed from two different angles, use one photo in the main catalog and switch to the second photo when customers open the perfume details. Do not create a duplicate product for the alternate angle.

Remove old multi-perfume group photos from the catalog display. Keep tidy existing single-perfume photos as the main image, adding newly supplied photos of that exact perfume to a swipeable gallery in its details. Do not substitute pictures of other scents from the same brand.

**Why:** The owner requested this arrangement explicitly.

**How to apply:** Compare perfume uploads by product identity and angle when assembling the catalog. Identical duplicate photos need only one copy.

For the four Lattafa perfumes, display the name, volume (75 ml), and price (55 ILS each) with each photo. Do not show color descriptions or a “per item” label.

**Why:** The owner clarified that the supplied colors were only for identifying the photos, not customer-facing copy; names and sizes must remain visible.

**How to apply:** Keep Cookie Crave, Vanilla Freak, Whipped Pleasure, and Berry On Top individually labeled on catalog cards.

Write all perfume names in English uppercase.

**Why:** The owner requested English CAPITAL names.

**How to apply:** Apply this to perfume names throughout the store, including the Arabic interface.
